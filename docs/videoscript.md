## 📋 Pre-Recording Setup Checklist

Before hitting record, prepare your browser tabs in this exact order:
1. **Tab 1 (Main App):** `http://localhost:3000/dashboard` (Dark/Modern Dashboard)
2. **Tab 2 (Live Facebook Page):** Your real connected Facebook Page showing a live published post created with MakeYourPost.
3. **Sample Content to Copy-Paste:**
   - *For Style Analyzer:* 2-3 short sample posts (e.g. tech founder advice or fitness tips).
   - *For AI Composer:* A punchy topic like *"5 timeless rules for solo founders building in public"*.

## 🎬 Screen-by-Screen Demo Script

---

### 🌟 Scene 1: The Hook & The "Creator Treadmill" Problem (0:00 – 0:35)

**🖥️ On Screen Action:**
- Start directly on the sleek **MakeYourPost Dashboard (`/dashboard`)**.
- Slowly move the cursor across the clean metrics, scheduled queue preview, and connected Facebook Page indicator.

**🎙️ Spoken Voiceover:**
> *"Hi everyone! I’m **Shrabon**, and this is **MakeYourPost** — an autonomous, agentic social media platform built for modern creators and brands.*
>
> *Running an active social media page sounds simple, but in reality, creators are trapped on a 5-step treadmill every single day: researching ideas, writing engaging copy, designing graphic posters, making memes, scheduling, and analyzing what works.*
>
> *Doing this manually takes hours, and juggling 5 different AI tools creates fragmented chaos. We asked: **What if an autonomous AI agent could handle this entire end-to-end workflow on autopilot?**"*

---

### 🌟 Scene 2: High-Level Agentic Concept (0:35 – 1:00)

**🖥️ On Screen Action:**
- Hover over the sidebar navigation: **Style Analyzer**, **AI Personas**, **Composer**, **Poster Templates**, **Meme Studio**, and **Scheduled Slots**.

**🎙️ Spoken Voiceover:**
> *"At the core of MakeYourPost are **AI Personas** — specialized autonomous agents tailored to your specific niche and tone.*
>
> *These agents don't just generate generic text. They are complete creative directors: drafting copy, designing high-resolution graphic posters in our **Poster Lab**, and generating viral memes in our **Meme Studio**.*
>
> *You can use these tools interactively, or let your personas manage your page **100% autonomously**.*
>
> *Let’s see how it works in real-time."*

---

### 🌟 Scene 3: The Secret Weapon — Style Analyzer (1:00 – 1:35)

**🖥️ On Screen Action:**
- Click on **Style Analyzer** in the sidebar (or navigate to `/dashboard/style-analyzer`).
- Paste 2 sample posts into the input box.
- Click **Analyze Style**.
- Highlight the real-time breakdown appearing on screen: **Tone Tags**, **Sentence Structure**, **Emoji Usage**, **Vocabulary Level**, and the generated **Persona Profile**.
- Click **Save as AI Persona**.

**🎙️ Spoken Voiceover:**
> *"Every creator wants their AI to sound like **them** — not a generic chatbot. Or, if you're starting fresh, you want to emulate the top-performing creators in your niche.*
>
> *That’s where our **Style Analyzer** comes in.*
>
> *Just paste a few previous posts. The engine analyzes tone, sentence cadence, vocabulary, and emoji habits, reverse-engineering the creator’s unique voice into a complete **AI Persona DNA** with a single click.*
>
> *Now, the AI doesn't just know what to say — it knows exactly **how you say it**."*

---

### 🌟 Scene 4: AI Personas & Strategy Control Center (1:35 – 2:05)

**🖥️ On Screen Action:**
- Click on **AI Personas & Prompts (`/dashboard/ai-settings`)**.
- Expand a persona card (e.g. *Tech Founder* or *Health & Fitness*).
- Point the mouse at **Niche**, **Target Audience**, **Tone Tags**, and the **Model Selector** (GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Mistral, Gemini).
- Scroll down and highlight the **Learned Strategy & Insights** panel.

**🎙️ Spoken Voiceover:**
> *"Here in **AI Personas**, you manage your autonomous agents.*
>
> *You can configure distinct personas for different pages — setting their niche, audience, and custom prompt rules.*
>
> *You can switch the underlying intelligence per persona between **Claude, GPT-4o, Gemini, or Mistral**.*
>
> *And look at this **Learned Strategy** panel: the system periodically monitors your live Facebook reach and engagement, automatically distilling performance insights directly into the persona’s prompt so it continuously gets smarter over time."*

---

### 🌟 Scene 5: Visual Excellence — Poster Lab & Meme Studio (2:05 – 2:45)

**🖥️ On Screen Action:**
- Navigate to **Poster Templates (`/dashboard/templates`)**.
- Click through 2 layout archetypes (e.g. Minimalist Tech, Quote Card, Bold Badge layout).
- Hover over the text overlay, badge icon, and background image to show crisp rendering.
- Switch to **Meme Studio (`/dashboard/memes`)** in the sidebar.
- Click **Generate AI Meme** to show a witty top/bottom punchline dynamically rendered on a trending meme template.

**🎙️ Spoken Voiceover:**
> *"Now for the visual problem. Plain text gets ignored on social feeds, but standard generative AI image tools notoriously butcher typography and spelling.*
>
> *So we built a **Deterministic Poster Rendering Engine**.*
>
> *The AI acts as the **Art Director** — choosing color schemes, typography pairings, vector badges, and layout hierarchy based on the topic. Our engine dynamically shrinks text to fit safe zones and calculates **WCAG contrast ratios** to guarantee 100% readable, professional graphics every time.*
>
> *And for viral engagement, our **Meme Studio** pairs trending meme templates with niche-specific AI punchlines in seconds."*

---

>

### 🌟 Scene 7: The "WOW" Moment — Autonomous Scheduler & Live Facebook Publishing (3:15 – 3:45)

- **The Climax:** Click the **"View on Facebook"** link or switch directly to your open **Facebook Page tab** on `facebook.com`.

> *In **Scheduled Posts**, our background worker schedules daily time slots. When a slot triggers, the persona autonomously researches the topic, writes the post, renders the companion poster, and publishes directly to Facebook using the official **Meta Graph API**.*
>
> *This isn't a prototype mockup. Look at our **live Facebook Page** right here — this post was written, designed, and published completely hands-free by MakeYourPost!"*

1. **True Agentic AI (Not Just a Wrapper):**
   - Multi-step reasoning: Style Analysis → Persona Synthesis → Topic Ideation → Copywriting → Deterministic Design → Self-Critique Quality Pass → Autonomous Scheduling → Feedback Learning Loop.

   - Live analytics are synthesized into persona system prompts for continuous reinforcement learning.

## 💡 Quick Tips for Recording
- **Tone:** Energetic, confident, crisp, and proud.
- **Mouse Movement:** Smooth and deliberate (avoid shaking the mouse or rapid clicking).
- **Audio:** Use a clear microphone; keep background noise to zero.
- **Pacing:** Let key moments breathe (pause for 1 second after showing the live Facebook post so judges can see the real graphic!).
